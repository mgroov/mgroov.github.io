Masters Project

My masters project was a thesis studying the effects of procedural generation on user immersion. To accomplish this I created a prototype game in Godot that generated two seperate conditions. I then had a series of users run through these conditions to help me understand the impact or lack of impact on their immersion. The study revealed that procedural generation conditions can have an impact on user immersion.
